Prompts for MANDALA & NATURE PATTERNS
Each prompt makes one square picture. Pages marked [reference] should get the
character's picture attached as a reference image.

=== cover  ->  art/cover.png
Adult coloring book illustration, flat black-and-white line art, clean digital drawing on a plain white background, square image.
A large circular mandala fills the frame, built from concentric rings of petals, teardrop shapes and lace-like filigree, radiating from a central flower bloom. Surrounding the mandala, trailing vines with layered leaves and small blossoms curl into the corners, each leaf textured with fine veins and tiny dot clusters filling remaining spaces.
Style: fine, elegant black outlines with rich, intricate detail; layered decorative patterns, flowing linework and lots of small enclosed shapes to color, in the style of a calm, relaxing adult coloring page; every shape closed; pure white fills; no shading. The drawing sits in the middle of a plain white square canvas with an even white margin around it.
Avoid: text, letters, words, numbers, title, signature, border, frame, shading, grey tones, gradients, hatching, texture, reflections, glare, sparkles, background scenery.
